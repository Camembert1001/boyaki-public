Do status, assignment, or reporting rules force you to correct things manually?

Examples include resetting a recurring item back to due, rotating the next assignee on a different schedule, duplicate entry, handoff corrections, or other work that exists only because two rules are coupled.

Write only your own underlying problem. BOYAKI does not automatically copy text from an external issue or support thread. What you submit here is published as your own BOYAKI. Do not include secrets, private workplace data, or personal information.

After a problem attracts a Solution Candidate, people with the same problem can participate as Problem owners, Testers, or Makers. BOYAKI is not a replacement support channel for the product where you found this link.
